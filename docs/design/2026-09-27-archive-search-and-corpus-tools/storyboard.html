Archive search, referencing and corpus tools: storyboard

Highlight Scout · ArchiveScout · WriteFlex · 27 September 2026 · drafts to correct, not decisions. Each green badge names a candidate so you can say "J4 is right, J7-B is wrong". Rough sketches: the boxes are placeholders, the words and numbers are the real ones from the journeys file.

Aims

  1. Find and cite. Anything I have written or saved, I can find in seconds and put into what I am writing, with where it came from.
  2. Complete without thinking. My archive has everything new, and I can tell at a glance when it doesn't.
  3. See the language. I can see how words and ideas behave across my corpora: in context, with their collocates, as recurring phrases, and over time.
  4. Understand any pile. Any folder of documents I point at becomes something I can survey, clean and query with the same tools.
  5. Ask and get evidence. I can ask a question of an archive and get an answer that cites the passages it rests on.

What we know

  • The sync is switched off, not broken. Readwise was last pulled on 22 July; it holds 732 highlights the archive lacks (72 July, 426 August, 234 September). Zotero was last pulled on 1 July. Installed 0.5.4; 0.5.5 was never installed.
  • Corpora: his writing, 1,506 pieces (about 2M words); tweets, 14,892; highlights, 14,724 works (X 9,683, Readwise 4,698, Zotero 343).
  • The corpus needs cleaning before counts can be trusted. Top n-grams include "amp amp amp" (266), "i don t" and "p tel daily"; Czech words split at every accented letter. Every corpus view needs a normalisation step.
