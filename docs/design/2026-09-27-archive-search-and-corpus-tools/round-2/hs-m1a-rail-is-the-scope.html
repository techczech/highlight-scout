HS-M1A

The rail is the scope

The HS-1B window, with the “Classic search” button gone. The rail decides what you are searching, and what you are searching decides which tools you see. Under All corpora the list stays grouped Writing / Tweets / Highlights with only Sort in its header. Pick Highlights, or X, Readwise or Zotero, and a slim bar appears above the results carrying today’s Classic toolbar in one line: Keyword / Semantic, match mode, the colour row, Tags, Filters and Rows. Group (with its “then” sub-group) and Sort move into the list header, above the rows they arrange; at 990 px the Classic toolbar and the colour row do not fit on one line.

Open design questions
